This is a shopkeeper getting paid thirty thousand naira in digital dollars. The customer scanned, the receipt printed. That's Kobo.

Shops in Nigeria that want dollars take them the hard way today: a wallet address pasted into WhatsApp. No amount, no confirmation, no receipt.

Kobo is a till. You type the price in naira, like you always have. It pulls today's rate and works out the USDC itself.

The customer scans with any Solana wallet. The rate is locked the moment the QR appears, so nobody argues about it later.

Kobo watches Solana for that exact payment, and prints the receipt. Share it to WhatsApp, with a link anyone can check on Solscan.

Kobo never touches the money. It goes straight to the shop's own wallet, and every receipt links to the payment on chain.

At the end of the day, the shop sees every sale and closes the day. Customers paying from a Seeker phone are spotted by their Genesis Token, and lined up for an SKR reward.

It connects through Mobile Wallet Adapter, so the shop's own wallet signs everything.
